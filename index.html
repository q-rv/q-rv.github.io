Rondel v1.600 · 3 styles

Rondel
<RV> Ring Coin

A geometric display typeface in which every letter, figure and symbol is drawn inside one circle, using only circle arcs and straight lines. Strokes that reach the edge are trimmed by the circle, so their ends are curved cuts lying on it.

  • Rondel (proportional, kerned), Ring and Coin (monospaced)
  • Monograms: type <RV> for a single medallion
  • Google Fonts Latin Core; best at 32 px and up
  • Submitted to Google Fonts

Fjellrune v2.000

Fjellrune
<RV> ᚠᛁᛖᛚᛚ

An angular Nordic display typeface inspired by Elder Futhark geometry and carved runestone lettering. Every glyph is built from straight, blade-cut strokes, and every long stave carries a signature axe nick cut into its leading edge.

  • Rune-shield medallions: type <RV> for an octagonal shield
  • Elder Futhark & Nordic Runic block (U+16A0–U+16F0)
  • Google Fonts Latin Core; best at 28 px and up
